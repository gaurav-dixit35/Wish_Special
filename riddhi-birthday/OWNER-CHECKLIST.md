## Your voice: 2 recordings still to come

These should be **your messages to Riddhi**, recorded in the language and tone
you naturally use with her. MP3 is the final format. If your phone provides
M4A/WAV, send that original and it can be prepared for the site.

| Recording | Where it plays | Purpose | Suggested length | Final file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main birthday message | C12, after the secrets collection and before the letter | Let her hear you personally wishing her, recalling a memory, and saying what her friendship means | 60–120 seconds | `others/voice/voice-note.mp3` |
| Secret bonus message | The hidden footer heart; replayable from C11 | Reward her curiosity with a short inside joke, affectionate tease, or a personal extra wish | 10–25 seconds | `others/voice/secret.mp3` |

The durations are suggestions, not playback limits. One main voice note is
enough; additional main clips are optional and can appear in the C12 playlist.
The secret clip remains separate from that playlist.

A natural structure for the main message:

1. Say her name and wish her a happy birthday.
2. Mention one specific memory, such as the bus, trek, or bootcamp.
3. Say what you appreciate about her and wish her something for the coming year.
4. End in your usual style: a nickname, a little tease, or your own sign-off.

The secret recording can be as simple as: “You found this too, Miss Alien?
Of course you did. One extra birthday wish, only for you…” Then add your own
joke or sentence. This is an optional idea, not text you must read.

Record somewhere quiet, hold the phone a steady distance from your mouth, and
leave out background music: the website already provides it and lowers its
volume while a voice plays. A clear, natural recording matters more than a
perfect performance. An optional written transcript of the main recording
can appear below its player for someone who cannot hear the audio. You may
also send the secret recording's wording if you want matching written copy.
