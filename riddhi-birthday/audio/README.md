# Local birthday audio

All eight files are supplied and validated:

- birthday-tune.mp3 — Happy Birthday To You, acoustic guitar
- pop.mp3
- whoosh.mp3
- sparkle.mp3
- blow.mp3
- match.mp3
- whistle.mp3
- snore.mp3

Creator credits, original download links, licenses, preparation, and durations
are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). These are real CC0 recordings, served locally.
Music starts after a tap, loops at a gentle level, follows global mute, and
lowers while personal voice notes play. The synthesized sounds remain only
as fallbacks if an optional audio file becomes unavailable.

The owner's main and secret voice recordings belong in `others/voice/` and
have not been supplied yet.
