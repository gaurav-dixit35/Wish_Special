The Freesound sources were downloaded using their publicly exposed high-quality
MP3 versions. Their licensed recordings are the sources of the prepared files;
no account-only original download was used.

All files use MP3 at 44.1 kHz and have been fully decoded with FFmpeg to check
that they contain valid, nonempty audio. Encoded MP3 container durations may
include a few milliseconds of encoder padding. Combined size: about **0.74 MiB**.
The existing site audio engine supplies looping, user-gesture playback, global
mute, and lower music volume while a personal voice note plays.

These public sound effects and music do not replace the owner's two personal
voice recordings in `others/voice/`.
