# Dog sticker sources

Ten local PNG stickers downloaded from FreeSVG and prepared for the birthday site.
Each linked source page identifies the illustration as **Public Domain**; FreeSVG supplies its art under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Source pages and transparent PNGs were checked on **4 October 2026**. No attribution is required by CC0; this record preserves provenance.

The artwork was cropped to its transparent bounds, fitted inside a 400 × 400 transparent canvas, and optimized as PNG. No watermark removal or generative edits were used.
